Install CyberSathi as a desktop app

Get a CyberSathi icon on your desktop, taskbar or home screen — open it like any installed program.

🖥️ Windows / Mac (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open this site in Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Look at the right side of the address bar for an install icon (⊕ or a small monitor).
  3. Click it, then click Install.
  4. A CyberSathi shortcut appears on your desktop. Pin it to taskbar from the right-click menu.

📱 Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm. The icon appears on your home screen.

🍏 iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.
Tip: After install, your CyberSathi opens in its own window — no browser tabs, no distractions. Perfect for the counter.
